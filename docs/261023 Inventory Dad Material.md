## Recipes (`DIY Cooking/Recipes/`)

- **The format, from his own notes (`index.docx`, `TheRecipes.odt`, `Template V4`):**
  - Each recipe has *Guided Steps* plus a *Quick Reference Card* (A6 / 4×6).
  - Guided Steps mark effort, duration, best body position and every chance for a break. No standing step is longer than 10 minutes.
  - **Line legend in `Template V4`:** no line = do the next step immediately · thin = within 5 min · dashed = within 2 min · thick = wait 30+ min (hours or days).
    **Decision (23 Oct):** the first batch of recipe sheets is newer, so where they differ, the first batch wins. The site keeps its legend: heavy line = safe to stop, dashed = optional part, shaded notes = pace.
  - **Shading:** the step is easily done seated at a table.
  - **Icons:** hourglass = wait · infinity = a place you can stop altogether · stopwatch = timed. Position marks are "over the work", "in your lap or at a small table", and "barstool or leaning aid".
  - **Spoons 1–5**, with his definitions: 1 = just eat, through to 5 = a whole project.
- **Full recipes:** Best Cornbread, 4-Day Sourdough, Liver n Onions, Mussels, Pickles, Potato Chips, Ranch Dip, Tortilla Chips, Pumpkin Snickerdoodles, Peach Wine, plus the updated Birria and Paella.
- **Quick Reference cards (A6):** Creamy Dips & Dressings, Hummus, Liver n Onions, Mussels, Pickles, Pork Belly, Potato Chips, Ranch, Sourdough, Tortilla Chips, Vinaigrettes.
- **Step photos:** `Recipes/images/` (45 of Dad's own photos).
- **Drafts and notes:** birria, chili, kimchi, menudo, salsas, chorizo, beignets, pulled pork, apple-pear sauce.
