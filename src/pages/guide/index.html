Reading a Label in Ten Seconds

  1. Skip the front of the pack. “Natural”, “wholesome” and “heart-healthy” are marketing. The facts are on the back.
  2. Read the ingredient list. Ingredients are listed by weight, most first. The first three tell you what you are mostly eating.
  3. Count and picture. If the list is long, or you can’t picture an ingredient growing or sitting in a kitchen, think twice.
