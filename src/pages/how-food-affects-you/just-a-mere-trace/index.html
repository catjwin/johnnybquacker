The Effects

Just a Mere Trace

How long does it take to prove that a little bit, every day, for years, adds up?

By Johnny B. Quacker

Proven, Eventually

Each of these was everywhere, in amounts we were told were too small to matter. Long-term exposure turned out to be dangerous.

  • Leaded gas
  • Leaded paint
  • Lead plumbing
  • Asbestos
  • Cigarettes and cigars
  • Snuff
  • Second-hand smoke

How long did each one take to be proven?

Today’s trace freak-outs are things like fluoride, 5G and plastic bottles. And yet there’s no freak-out about the most pervasive trace exposures of all: the ones in the food we eat every day.
