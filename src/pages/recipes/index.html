Remedy No. 3 · The Kitchen

Recipes

Short ingredient lists and plain methods. Nothing you need a chemist to pronounce.

Spoons

Every recipe is rated in spoons, from “spoon theory”: the way many people living with chronic illness count the energy a task will take. Fewer spoons, less effort. Each recipe says what changes the rating, like doing it in one go instead of in stages.

Cooking with Breaks

The steps are laid out so you can see where it’s safe to stop. Each step sits beside the ingredients it uses. A heavy line marks a place to rest, and notes tell you when not to walk away. Tick steps off as you go and the page remembers where you got to.

Every recipe has a helper card too: the short version, to print and keep by the stove once you know it.
