Overnight Steel-Cut Oats

Ten minutes the night before, a hot breakfast in the morning. No packet required.

Photo: bowl of oats with berries
  • Serves4
  • Evening5 min
  • Morning5–10 min
  • You need1 pan
Why It’s on the List

Steel-cut oats are whole oat kernels, chopped — nothing added. Starting them the night before takes the half-hour wait out of the morning.

Ingredients

  • Steel-cut oats1 cup
  • Water4 cups
  • Salta pinch
  • Berries or chopped fruit, to serve1 cup
Substitutions Whole milk for half the water, for creamier oats.
Top with nuts or a spoon of plain yogurt instead of fruit.

Method

  1. In the evening, bring the water and salt to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Stir in the oats and let them boil for 1 minute.
  3. Turn off the heat, put the lid on and leave the pan on the stove overnight.
  4. In the morning, warm the oats over medium-low heat for 5–10 minutes, stirring. Add a splash of water or milk if they’re too thick.
  5. Serve topped with the fruit. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 5 days.
At the Grocer’s The ingredient list should say one thing: oats. Skip the flavoured instant packets.
