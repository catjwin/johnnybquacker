The Only Vinaigrette You Need

Five ingredients, five minutes, one jar. Make it on Sunday and it’s there all week.

Photo: jar of dressing beside a bowl of leaves
  • Makes½ cup
  • Prep5 min
  • Keeps1 week
  • You need1 jar
Why It’s on the List

A bottle of dressing can carry a long ingredient list. This one has five, and you can see every one of them in the jar.

Ingredients

  • Extra-virgin olive oil6 tbsp
  • Red wine vinegar2 tbsp
  • Dijon mustard1 tsp
  • Small shallot, finely chopped1
  • Salta good pinch
Substitutions Lemon juice or sherry vinegar for the red wine vinegar.
A grated garlic clove for the shallot.

Method

  1. Put the shallot, vinegar and salt in a jar. Leave for 5 minutes to take the bite out of the shallot.
  2. Add the mustard and olive oil.
  3. Put the lid on and shake hard for 10 seconds, until it turns creamy.
  4. Taste it on a leaf, not a spoon. Add more salt or a splash more vinegar if it needs it.
  5. Keep it in the fridge. The oil firms up when cold, so let it sit out for 10 minutes and shake again before using.
At the Grocer’s Look for olive oil with a harvest date on the bottle, and buy a size you’ll finish in a couple of months.
— J.B.Q.
