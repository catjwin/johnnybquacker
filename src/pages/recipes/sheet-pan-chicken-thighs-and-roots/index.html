Remedy No. 3 · Supper

Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs & Roots

One tray, seven ingredients, no sauce from a bottle. The weeknight supper I come back to most.

Photo: the tray straight from the oven, on a wooden table
  • Serves4
  • Prep10 min
  • Cook35 min
  • You need1 tray
Why It’s on the List

Skin-on thighs stay juicy, cost less than breasts and need no marinade. Everything on the tray is a whole food — the label on each should list a single ingredient.

Ingredients

  • Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs8
  • Carrots, in 2-inch batons4
  • Parsnips, cut the same3
  • Red onions, in wedges2
  • Extra-virgin olive oil3 tbsp
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme4 sprigs
  • Salt and black pepperto taste
Substitutions No parsnips? Sweet potato or butternut squash.
No fresh herbs? 1 tsp dried, added with the oil.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss the carrots, parsnips and onion with 2 tbsp of the oil and a good pinch of salt and pepper. Spread them across a large rimmed tray in one layer.
  3. Pat the chicken dry — dry skin is crisp skin. Rub with the last tablespoon of oil, season well and nestle the thighs skin-side up among the vegetables. Tuck in the herbs.
  4. Roast for 35–40 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and the thickest thigh reads 165°F (74°C) on a thermometer.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, then spoon the pan juices over everything and serve from the tray.
At the Grocer’s The chicken label should say one thing: chicken. Skip packs that list “solution”, “broth” or “natural flavoring”.
— J.B.Q.
